Interpretation: Incident Database measures event-level incidents, while GDELT measures reporting and, while additional research has not been conducted, institutional responses, too. Where GDELT is matched to an actual incident (or a response to it), the matching rule uses a ±3-day window plus overlapping threat category or analytical family; this indicates correspondence rather than proving that an individual article describes the exact incident. Response intensity is an analytical weighting, not a measure of objective response effectiveness.
